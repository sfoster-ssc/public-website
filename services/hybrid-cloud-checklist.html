Why it matters

A hybrid stack can look stable while hiding gaps in recovery, ownership, or operational readiness. This checklist helps you identify weak points before they show up as downtime, failed restores, or audit surprises.

  • Confirm the environment can meet realistic recovery objectives (RTO/RPO).
  • Verify backups are restorable, not just scheduled.
  • Check failover paths, dependencies, and communication steps.
  • Ensure runbooks are current and accessible during an outage.

Discovery and scope

  • Confirm the systems, platforms, and applications in scope.
  • Identify the owners for infrastructure, storage, backup, and DR.
  • Map dependencies between applications, databases, DNS, identity, and network services.
  • Note which workloads are business-critical and which are lower priority.

Data protection

  • Verify backups are actually restorable.
  • Confirm backup frequency supports the required RPO.
  • Check retention policies and immutability protections.
  • Review replication lag and offsite copy status.
  • Confirm backup copies are isolated from production access.

Recovery readiness

  • Validate the documented RTO for each critical workload.
  • Test restore procedures from at least one recent point in time.
  • Confirm the correct boot order for dependent systems.
  • Verify DNS, authentication, and connectivity steps in a failover.
  • Document how failback works after the primary site is restored.

Security and operations

  • Confirm least-privilege access for recovery tasks.
  • Review alerting for backup failures and replication delays.
  • Confirm logging is available during and after a recovery event.
  • Verify that runbooks are current and accessible during an outage.
  • Make sure the right people can declare a disaster and trigger recovery.
